About TaskFlow

TaskFlow is a lightweight task-management web application designed to help you organize, track, and complete your daily tasks efficiently.

How to Use TaskFlow

  1. Go to the Home page to view a summary of your tasks.
  2. Click Add Task to create a new task with a title, description, due date, and priority.
  3. Visit Task List to see all your tasks, and use the filter buttons to view All, Pending, or Completed tasks.
  4. Click on a task to open Task Detail, where you can mark it as complete or delete it.
